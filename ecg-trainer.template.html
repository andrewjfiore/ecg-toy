ECG Trainer 12-lead practice

How this works

Practice reading 12-lead ECGs. Every case is drawn fresh, so you can keep going as long as you like without working through a fixed set you start to recognize.

Each case

  • Read the short history, then the tracing.
  • Choose the diagnosis from five options.
  • Submit to see the answer, which leads gave it away, and a teaching point.

What counts

Only your diagnosis. Your score, your streak and the case you get next all come from that, and nothing else.

Two modes

  • Easy asks for the diagnosis. You can still mark leads as you think, but it is just a scratchpad.
  • Hard also asks you to call all 12 leads before you submit. Those calls are shown separately and never change your score.

Marking leads

Click a lead to call it abnormal, then normal, then clear it again. The keyboard works too: tab to a lead and press Enter.

Some leads are a genuine matter of judgment. Those are left out of your lead accuracy rather than counted against you.

Your progress

Everything stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and it keeps working with no connection. You can export or clear it from the Progress tab.

One caveat

The tracings are simulated for teaching. They are not recordings from real patients, so practice on them freely but do not treat anything here as a clinical reference.
